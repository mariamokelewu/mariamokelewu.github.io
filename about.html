About

How I got here.

My journey has been shaped by a simple curiosity: how businesses work, how rules shape them, and how economic systems create the conditions for people and businesses to thrive.

01
Where it began

Growing up around business.

Both of my parents are successful entrepreneurs. I was invested in how businesses worked — how they made a profit, what made one successful, and how wealth was created.

Wider lens

Looking behind the rules.

I initially approached these questions through law, but I was more invested in the commercial decisions and incentives sitting behind them.

Evidence in practice

Questions meet real businesses.

My legal and compliance experience gave me different ways of observing how businesses operate, respond to regulation, and manage risk in practice.

What I am moving toward

Economics and public policy.

I am interested in how economies generate wealth, what shapes who gets the opportunity to create it, and how policy choices influence business growth, resilience, and long-term development.

Connecting the threads

I see law, business, compliance, economics and public policy as connected disciplines.
