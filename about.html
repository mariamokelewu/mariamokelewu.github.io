About

My career began in law, but I quickly found myself more interested in the businesses sitting behind the legal questions: how they operate, how they make decisions, the risks they take and the environments that allow them to succeed or fail.

Since then, I have worked across legal operations, commercial operations, compliance, governance and risk. I currently work at HMRC, where I assess the financial and operational risks of UK businesses and use evidence to understand where intervention may be necessary.

I am increasingly interested in how regulation shapes business behaviour, how economic policy affects businesses and individuals, and how governments can create environments in which businesses can both comply and thrive.

My background is multidisciplinary. I intend to keep it that way.

This website is a collection of my professional experience, research, ideas, and the questions I am exploring as I continue to develop in that direction.
