01 — Practice & Perspective

Different vantage points. One continuing question.

My work has moved across law, commercial operations, and compliance. Each has changed what I look for when trying to understand a decision, a risk, or a rule in practice.

The thread through my work

I am interested in the space between what is intended, what is happening, and what the evidence allows us to conclude.

That means looking at more than the rule or the first explanation of a problem. It means asking what the records show, what context changes their meaning, what assumptions remain, and whether the proposed response fits the circumstances.

A note from the margins

The same requirement can be routine for one business and a serious drain on another. The rule may be identical; the capacity to absorb it is not.

A question I keep open

If a policy is achieving its stated purpose, what else should we examine before deciding whether it is working well?

See the approach in practice

Analysis is not just reaching an answer. It is understanding why that answer is justified.
