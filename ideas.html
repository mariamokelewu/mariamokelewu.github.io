02 — Ideas in Motion

Questions worth staying with.

Writing is one way I work through questions that emerge from practice, reading, and observation. I do not always begin with a conclusion. Sometimes I begin with a tension I want to understand better.

A recurring question

How do businesses exist within the economic environments created around them?

These essays explore different parts of that relationship: regulation and the room to grow, and the systems we rely on to protect us. They are not answers to every question. They are attempts to examine the questions more carefully.
