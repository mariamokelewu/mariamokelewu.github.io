Privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 21 September 2026.

Who I am

This website is operated by Mariam Okelewu. If you have a privacy question, you can contact me through the contact page.

What information I collect

If you use the contact form, I collect the information you choose to provide, currently your name, email address, subject and message. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the form unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. This processing is carried out by GitHub as part of its hosting service. GitHub Pages information.

Why I use your information

I use contact-form information to read, manage and respond to professional enquiries and related correspondence. My intended lawful basis for this processing is legitimate interests: responding to people who choose to contact me and managing professional communications.

Contact form provider

The contact form is processed through Basin, a third-party form service. Basin states that customers act as the data controller for personal data submitted through their forms and that Basin acts as a data processor. Basin stores submissions so they can appear in the dashboard and supports configurable retention and deletion. Basin GDPR information.

Basin is operated from Canada and its documentation states that some processing may involve US-based subprocessors. International transfers are handled under the mechanisms described in Basin's current privacy and data-processing documentation. Basin security and compliance information.

How long I keep contact enquiries

I keep contact information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, maintain relevant professional correspondence, and deal with any follow-up or legal obligations. Basin also applies the retention settings configured for the form; its current documentation states that retention can be set per form and that plan limits vary. Basin retention information.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not currently use Google Analytics or other optional analytics tools. If optional analytics is introduced in future, this notice will be updated and consent will be requested where required.

Who receives information

Contact-form information may be processed by Basin as the form provider and, where applicable, its subprocessors. Hosting-related technical information may be processed by GitHub.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the applicable law, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent. You can exercise applicable rights or ask a privacy question through the contact page.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how your personal information is being handled, please contact me first so I can try to resolve the issue. You also have the right to complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). The ICO's guidance explains the information that privacy notices should provide, including purposes, lawful bases, recipients, retention and individual rights. ICO privacy information guidance.

Changes to this notice

I may update this notice when the website, the services it uses or the way personal information is processed changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
